**NEET UG 2026 Biology – Notes from NCERT**

**NCERT chapters covered:** Class XII – Chapter 1 (Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants), Chapter 2 (Human Reproduction), Chapter 3 (Reproductive Health)

**NEET syllabus topics in this unit:**

1. **Sexual reproduction in flowering plants:** flower structure; development of male and female gametophytes; pollination – types, agencies and examples; outbreeding devices; pollen–pistil interaction; double fertilisation; post-fertilisation events – development of endosperm and embryo, development of seed and formation of fruit; special modes – apomixis, parthenocarpy, polyembryony; significance of seed and fruit formation
2. **Human reproduction:** male and female reproductive systems; microscopic anatomy of testis and ovary; gametogenesis – spermatogenesis and oogenesis; menstrual cycle; fertilisation, embryo development up to blastocyst formation, implantation; pregnancy and placenta formation (elementary idea); parturition (elementary idea); lactation (elementary idea)
3. **Reproductive health:** need for reproductive health and prevention of sexually transmitted diseases (STDs); birth control – need and methods, contraception and medical termination of pregnancy (MTP); amniocentesis; infertility and assisted reproductive technologies – IVF, ZIFT, GIFT (elementary idea for general awareness)

> **NEET Focus: How this unit is tested**
> - Very high weightage in Class XII Botany and Zoology. Many "ploidy" questions (nucellus, MMC, megaspore, embryo sac, PEN, endosperm, zygote) and "number" questions (7-celled 8-nucleate embryo sac, 250 lobules, 60,000–80,000 follicles, 200–300 million sperms).
> - Anther wall layers and tapetum; sporopollenin; 2-celled vs 3-celled pollen; filiform apparatus; outbreeding devices (monoecy prevents autogamy but not geitonogamy; dioecy prevents both).
> - Human reproduction: sequence of sperm ducts; Sertoli vs Leydig cells; hormonal control of spermatogenesis and menstrual cycle (LH surge); hormones of placenta; where fertilisation occurs.
> - Reproductive health: IUD types, mode of action of pills, Saheli, MTP Act timelines, which STIs are not curable, ZIFT vs IUT vs GIFT vs ICSI vs IUI.

## Quick Revision – High-Yield One-liners

- Anther: bilobed, dithecous, tetrasporangiate. Microsporangium wall: epidermis, endothecium, middle layers, tapetum.
- Tapetum nourishes pollen; cells multinucleate with dense cytoplasm.
- Exine – sporopollenin (most resistant; no enzyme degrades it); germ pores lack sporopollenin. Intine – cellulose and pectin.
- Pollen 25–50 µm; >60% of angiosperms shed pollen at 2-celled stage.
- Rice, wheat pollen viable about 30 min; pollen stored at −196°C in liquid nitrogen.
- *Parthenium* came with imported wheat; causes pollen allergy.
- MMC in micropylar region of nucellus. Monosporic embryo sac: 7-celled, 8-nucleate.
- Filiform apparatus of synergids guides pollen tube.
- Cleistogamous flowers: *Viola, Oxalis, Commelina*.
- Geitonogamy – functionally cross, genetically self. Xenogamy – only genetically different pollen.
- Water pollination: *Vallisneria, Hydrilla, Zostera* (about 30 genera, mostly monocots).
- *Amorphophallus* – tallest flower (about 6 ft). *Yucca*–moth mutualism.
- Monoecious (castor, maize) prevents autogamy only; dioecious (papaya) prevents both.
- Emasculation – remove anthers before dehiscence; bagging with butter paper.
- Double fertilisation = syngamy + triple fusion; PEN is 3n.
- Tender coconut water = free-nuclear endosperm; kernel = cellular endosperm.
- Embryo stages: proembryo → globular → heart-shaped → mature.
- Scutellum – cotyledon of grass; coleoptile covers shoot apex; coleorrhiza covers radicle.
- Perisperm – black pepper, beet. Albuminous – wheat, maize, barley, castor. Non-albuminous – pea, groundnut.
- False fruits – apple, strawberry, cashew. Parthenocarpic – banana.
- Oldest viable seed – *Lupinus arcticus* (10,000 years); date palm 2000 years.
- Apomixis – Asteraceae, grasses; nucellar polyembryony – *Citrus*, mango.
- Testes 2–2.5°C below body temperature; 250 lobules; 1–3 seminiferous tubules per lobule.
- Sertoli – nutrition; Leydig – androgens. LH → Leydig; FSH → Sertoli.
- Seminal plasma rich in fructose, calcium, enzymes.
- 200–300 million sperms per ejaculate; 60% normal shape, 40% vigorous motility.
- Fallopian tube 10–12 cm: infundibulum (fimbriae) → ampulla → isthmus.
- Uterus: perimetrium, myometrium, endometrium.
- Mammary gland: 15–20 lobes; alveoli → tubules → duct → ampulla → lactiferous duct.
- 60,000–80,000 primary follicles per ovary at puberty. Antrum in tertiary follicle.
- Menstrual cycle 28/29 days; menstrual phase 3–5 days; LH surge on day 14.
- Fertilisation in ampulla; morula = 8–16 blastomeres; blastocyst = trophoblast + inner cell mass.
- Placental hormones: hCG, hPL, estrogens, progestogens. Relaxin from ovary. hCG, hPL, relaxin only in pregnancy.
- Heart forms after 1 month; first trimester 12 weeks; foetal movements in 5th month.
- Foetal ejection reflex → oxytocin (positive feedback).
- Colostrum – antibodies.
- Family planning started 1951; RCH programme; amniocentesis banned for sex determination.
- Marriageable age: female 18, male 21.
- Periodic abstinence days 10–17; lactational amenorrhea up to 6 months.
- Pills: 21 days + 7-day gap; Saheli – weekly, non-steroidal.
- Emergency contraception within 72 hours.
- MTP legalised 1971; MTP Amendment Act 2017: 12 weeks – one doctor; 12–24 weeks – two doctors.
- Not curable STIs: hepatitis-B, genital herpes, HIV. High incidence: 15–24 years.
- Infertility – no conception after 2 years of unprotected cohabitation.

## Practice Questions (NEET pattern)

1. The innermost wall layer of the microsporangium that nourishes the developing pollen is:
   - (a) Endothecium
   - (b) Middle layer
   - (c) Epidermis
   - (d) Tapetum
2. Sporopollenin is present in:
   - (a) Intine
   - (b) Generative cell wall
   - (c) Germ pores
   - (d) Exine, except at germ pores
3. A mature embryo sac of a typical angiosperm is:
   - (a) 8-celled, 8-nucleate
   - (b) 8-celled, 7-nucleate
   - (c) 7-celled, 7-nucleate
   - (d) 7-celled, 8-nucleate
4. The ploidy of the primary endosperm nucleus is:
   - (a) 3n
   - (b) 2n
   - (c) n
   - (d) 4n
5. Which condition prevents both autogamy and geitonogamy?
   - (a) Monoecy
   - (b) Cleistogamy
   - (c) Dioecy
   - (d) Protandry
6. Perisperm is found in:
   - (a) Castor
   - (b) Black pepper
   - (c) Wheat
   - (d) Pea
7. Leydig cells secrete:
   - (a) Inhibin
   - (b) Androgens
   - (c) Nutrients for sperms
   - (d) FSH
8. In human females, meiosis I is completed:
   - (a) Before birth
   - (b) In the primary follicle
   - (c) After fertilisation
   - (d) In the tertiary follicle, before ovulation
9. Ovulation is induced by:
   - (a) FSH surge
   - (b) Estrogen fall
   - (c) Progesterone
   - (d) LH surge
10. Which hormone is NOT produced by the placenta?
    - (a) hCG
    - (b) hPL
    - (c) Progestogens
    - (d) Relaxin
11. Which IUD is hormone-releasing?
    - (a) Lippes loop
    - (b) CuT
    - (c) LNG-20
    - (d) Multiload 375
12. In ZIFT, the embryo is transferred into:
    - (a) Uterus, with more than 8 blastomeres
    - (b) Vagina
    - (c) Fallopian tube, with up to 8 blastomeres
    - (d) Cervix
13. Which STI is NOT completely curable?
    - (a) Gonorrhoea
    - (b) Genital herpes
    - (c) Syphilis
    - (d) Trichomoniasis
14. According to the MTP (Amendment) Act 2017, termination between 12 and 24 weeks requires the opinion of:
    - (a) One registered medical practitioner
    - (b) Two registered medical practitioners
    - (c) A court order
    - (d) Not permitted

**Answers:** 1–d, 2–d, 3–d, 4–a, 5–c, 6–b, 7–b, 8–d, 9–d, 10–d, 11–c, 12–c, 13–b, 14–b
