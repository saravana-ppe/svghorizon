**NEET UG 2026 Biology – Notes from NCERT**

**NCERT chapters covered:** Class XI – Chapter 14 (Breathing and Exchange of Gases), Chapter 15 (Body Fluids and Circulation), Chapter 16 (Excretory Products and their Elimination), Chapter 17 (Locomotion and Movement), Chapter 18 (Neural Control and Coordination), Chapter 19 (Chemical Coordination and Integration)

**NEET syllabus topics in this unit:**

1. **Breathing and respiration:** respiratory organs in animals (recall only); respiratory system in humans; mechanism of breathing and its regulation; exchange of gases, transport of gases and regulation of respiration; respiratory volumes; disorders – asthma, emphysema, occupational respiratory disorders
2. **Body fluids and circulation:** composition of blood, blood groups, coagulation; composition of lymph and its function; human circulatory system – structure of heart and blood vessels; cardiac cycle, cardiac output, ECG; double circulation; regulation of cardiac activity; disorders – hypertension, coronary artery disease, angina pectoris, heart failure
3. **Excretory products and their elimination:** ammonotelism, ureotelism, uricotelism; human excretory system; urine formation; osmoregulation; regulation of kidney function – renin-angiotensin, ANF, ADH and diabetes insipidus; role of other organs; disorders – uraemia, renal failure, renal calculi, nephritis; dialysis and artificial kidney
4. **Locomotion and movement:** types of movement – ciliary, flagellar, muscular; skeletal muscle – contractile proteins and muscle contraction; skeletal system and its functions; joints; disorders – myasthenia gravis, tetany, muscular dystrophy, arthritis, osteoporosis, gout
5. **Neural control and coordination:** neuron and nerves; nervous system in humans – central, peripheral and visceral nervous system; generation and conduction of nerve impulse
6. **Chemical coordination and regulation:** endocrine glands and hormones; human endocrine system – hypothalamus, pituitary, pineal, thyroid, parathyroid, adrenal, pancreas, gonads; mechanism of hormone action (elementary idea); role of hormones as messengers and regulators; hypo- and hyperactivity and related disorders (dwarfism, acromegaly, cretinism, goitre, exophthalmic goitre, diabetes, Addison's disease)

*(Diseases and disorders are to be dealt with in brief, as per the syllabus.)*

> **NEET Focus: How this unit is tested**
> - This is the largest unit of Class XI Zoology. NEET asks many **numerical-value questions**: respiratory volumes, partial pressures, blood cell counts, WBC percentages, cardiac output, GFR, osmolarity, number of bones, vertebrae, ribs.
> - Sequence questions: path of air, cardiac cycle events, conduction of impulse in heart, urine formation, muscle contraction steps, nerve impulse.
> - Matching: hormone – gland – function – disorder; WBC type – function; joint – example; nephron part – function; brain part – function.
> - Assertion–reason: e.g., "SAN is the pacemaker because it generates maximum action potentials."

## Quick Revision – High-Yield One-liners

- Trachea divides at **5th thoracic vertebra**. Incomplete cartilaginous rings.
- Breathing rate **12–16 per minute**. TV **500 mL**; IRV 2500–3000; ERV 1000–1100; RV 1100–1200.
- VC = TV + IRV + ERV; TLC = VC + RV; FRC = ERV + RV.
- pO<sub>2</sub>: atmosphere 159, alveoli 104, deoxygenated blood 40, oxygenated 95, tissues 40.
- pCO<sub>2</sub>: atmosphere 0.3, alveoli 40, deoxygenated 45, oxygenated 40, tissues 45.
- CO<sub>2</sub> solubility 20–25 times O<sub>2</sub>.
- O<sub>2</sub> transport: 97% Hb, 3% plasma. CO<sub>2</sub>: 70% bicarbonate, 20–25% carbamino-Hb, 7% plasma.
- 100 mL oxygenated blood gives 5 mL O<sub>2</sub>; 100 mL deoxygenated blood gives 4 mL CO<sub>2</sub>.
- Respiratory rhythm centre – medulla; pneumotaxic – pons; O<sub>2</sub> role insignificant.
- Plasma 55%; formed elements 45%. Plasma proteins 6–8%.
- RBC 5–5.5 million/mm<sup>3</sup>; lifespan 120 days; Hb 12–16 g/100 mL; spleen = graveyard.
- WBC 6000–8000/mm<sup>3</sup>; neutrophils 60–65%, basophils 0.5–1%.
- Platelets 1.5–3.5 lakh/mm<sup>3</sup>; from megakaryocytes.
- Rh+ve about 80%; erythroblastosis foetalis prevented by anti-Rh antibodies after first delivery.
- Prothrombin → thrombin (by thrombokinase); fibrinogen → fibrin (by thrombin); Ca<sup>++</sup> essential.
- SAN = pacemaker (70–75 per minute). Cardiac cycle 0.8 s; stroke volume 70 mL; cardiac output 5 L/min.
- Lub – AV valves close; dub – semilunar valves close.
- P – atrial depolarisation; QRS – ventricular depolarisation; T – ventricular repolarisation.
- Heart is myogenic; sympathetic ↑, parasympathetic ↓.
- Normal BP 120/80; hypertension ≥ 140/90.
- Kidneys: T12 to L3 level; 10–12 cm; 120–170 g; about 1 million nephrons each.
- GFR 125 mL/min = 180 L/day; 99% reabsorbed; urine 1–1.5 L/day; pH 6.0; urea 25–30 g/day.
- Podocytes – filtration slits. JGA – renin.
- Medullary osmolarity 300 → 1200 mOsmol L<sup>−1</sup>; urine up to 4 times concentrated.
- Lungs remove about 200 mL CO<sub>2</sub> per minute.
- Muscles 40–50% of body weight. Sarcomere between two Z-lines. A-band unchanged in contraction.
- Troponin masks myosin binding sites; Ca<sup>++</sup> unmasks; myosin head = ATPase.
- 206 bones; axial 80; skull 22 (8 cranial + 14 facial); 26 vertebrae; 12 pairs of ribs.
- Each limb 30 bones; carpals 8, tarsals 7, phalanges 14 each.
- Femur – longest bone. Pivot – atlas/axis; saddle – thumb carpal/metacarpal.
- Na<sup>+</sup>/K<sup>+</sup> pump: 3 Na<sup>+</sup> out, 2 K<sup>+</sup> in. Resting membrane: outside +, inside −.
- Multipolar – cerebral cortex; bipolar – retina; unipolar – embryo.
- Corpus callosum connects hemispheres. Corpora quadrigemina – midbrain.
- Pituitary in sella tursica; oxytocin and ADH made by hypothalamus.
- Islets of Langerhans 1–2 million, 1–2% of pancreas; α – glucagon, β – insulin.
- Leydig cells – testosterone. Corpus luteum – progesterone.
- Erythropoietin – JG cells of kidney. ANF – atria.

## Practice Questions (NEET pattern)

1. Vital capacity of lungs is equal to:
   - (a) IRV + ERV + TV
   - (b) TV + ERV
   - (c) TV + IRV
   - (d) IRV + ERV + TV + RV
2. The partial pressure of oxygen in the alveoli is about:
   - (a) 159 mm Hg
   - (b) 95 mm Hg
   - (c) 104 mm Hg
   - (d) 40 mm Hg
3. About 70% of CO<sub>2</sub> is transported in blood as:
   - (a) Bicarbonate
   - (b) Dissolved CO<sub>2</sub>
   - (c) Carbamino-haemoglobin
   - (d) Carbonic acid
4. Which WBCs secrete histamine, serotonin and heparin?
   - (a) Neutrophils
   - (b) Eosinophils
   - (c) Monocytes
   - (d) Basophils
5. The QRS complex in an ECG represents:
   - (a) Atrial depolarisation
   - (b) Atrial repolarisation
   - (c) Ventricular repolarisation
   - (d) Ventricular depolarisation
6. The first heart sound "lub" is due to:
   - (a) Closure of tricuspid and bicuspid valves
   - (b) Closure of semilunar valves
   - (c) Opening of AV valves
   - (d) Atrial systole
7. Which part of the nephron is impermeable to water?
   - (a) Ascending limb of Henle's loop
   - (b) Descending limb of Henle's loop
   - (c) PCT
   - (d) Collecting duct
8. ANF acts as a check on the renin–angiotensin mechanism by:
   - (a) Vasodilation
   - (b) Vasoconstriction
   - (c) Increasing aldosterone
   - (d) Increasing ADH
9. During muscle contraction, which of the following does NOT change in length?
   - (a) I-band
   - (b) H-zone
   - (c) Sarcomere
   - (d) A-band
10. The joint between atlas and axis is a:
    - (a) Hinge joint
    - (b) Gliding joint
    - (c) Saddle joint
    - (d) Pivot joint
11. Bipolar neurons are found in:
    - (a) Cerebral cortex
    - (b) Embryonic stage only
    - (c) Spinal cord
    - (d) Retina
12. Which hormone is hypercalcemic?
    - (a) Thyrocalcitonin
    - (b) PTH
    - (c) Insulin
    - (d) Glucagon
13. Exophthalmic goitre is associated with:
    - (a) Hyperthyroidism
    - (b) Hypothyroidism
    - (c) Hypoparathyroidism
    - (d) Adrenal insufficiency
14. Steroid hormones mostly act through:
    - (a) Membrane-bound receptors and cAMP
    - (b) Ion channels only
    - (c) IP<sub>3</sub> as second messenger
    - (d) Intracellular receptors and gene expression

**Answers:** 1–a, 2–c, 3–a, 4–d, 5–d, 6–a, 7–a, 8–a, 9–d, 10–d, 11–d, 12–b, 13–a, 14–d
