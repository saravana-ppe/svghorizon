**NEET UG 2026 Biology – Notes from NCERT**

**NCERT chapters covered:** Class XI – Chapter 1 (The Living World), Chapter 2 (Biological Classification), Chapter 3 (Plant Kingdom), Chapter 4 (Animal Kingdom)

**NEET syllabus topics in this unit:**

1. What is living?; Biodiversity; Need for classification; Taxonomy and Systematics; Concept of species and taxonomical hierarchy; Binomial nomenclature
2. Five kingdom classification; salient features and classification of Monera, Protista and Fungi into major groups; Lichens; Viruses and Viroids
3. Salient features and classification of plants into major groups – Algae, Bryophytes, Pteridophytes, Gymnosperms (three to five salient and distinguishing features and at least two examples of each)
4. Salient features and classification of animals – non-chordates up to phyla level and chordates up to class level (three to five salient features and at least two examples)

> **NEET Focus: How this unit is tested**
> - This is a high-scoring, fact-based unit. Questions test examples ("Which of these is a sponge?"), matching (organism – group – feature), and exceptions.
> - Most asked areas: five-kingdom table, archaebacteria and cyanobacteria, protist groups, fungal classes with examples, algae table (pigment, food, flagella), bryophyte and pteridophyte life cycles, heterospory, phylum features and examples, chordate classes.
> - Learn every example printed in NCERT with its common name. NEET often picks the example directly from the NCERT text.

## Quick Revision – High-Yield One-liners

- Number of described species: **1.7–1.8 million**.
- Binomial nomenclature: **Linnaeus**. Codes: **ICBN** (plants), **ICZN** (animals).
- Lowest taxonomic category: **species**. Plants use **Division** instead of phylum.
- Five kingdom classification: **R.H. Whittaker (1969)**.
- Chitin cell wall: **Fungi**. Non-cellulosic (polysaccharide + amino acid) wall: **Monera**.
- Methane from cattle dung: **methanogens** (archaebacteria).
- Heterocysts: *Nostoc*, *Anabaena*.
- Smallest living cells; no cell wall: **Mycoplasma**.
- Diatomaceous earth: **diatoms** (silica walls).
- Red tides: *Gonyaulax* (dinoflagellate).
- Pellicle, mixotrophic: *Euglena*.
- Sleeping sickness: *Trypanosoma*. Malaria: *Plasmodium*.
- Dikaryophase: **Ascomycetes and Basidiomycetes**.
- Aseptate coenocytic mycelium: **Phycomycetes**.
- Imperfect fungi: **Deuteromycetes** – *Alternaria*, *Colletotrichum*, *Trichoderma*.
- *Neurospora*: biochemical and genetic work.
- Viruses named by **Beijerinck**; crystallised by **Stanley**; TMV mosaic by **Ivanowsky**.
- Viroid (free RNA): **T.O. Diener, 1971**, potato spindle tuber disease.
- Prions: **mad cow disease (BSE)**, **CJD**.
- Lichens: phycobiont + mycobiont; pollution indicators.
- Algin: brown algae. Carrageen: red algae. Agar: *Gelidium*, *Gracilaria*.
- Food supplement for space travellers: *Chlorella*.
- Floridean starch: red algae. Laminarin/mannitol: brown algae.
- Amphibians of plant kingdom: **bryophytes**. Peat: *Sphagnum*.
- Gemma cups: *Marchantia* (liverwort). Protonema: mosses.
- First vascular plants: **pteridophytes**. Heterosporous: *Selaginella*, *Salvinia*.
- Coralloid roots with cyanobacteria: *Cycas*. Mycorrhiza: *Pinus*.
- Tallest gymnosperm: *Sequoia*. Smallest angiosperm: *Wolffia*. Tall angiosperm: *Eucalyptus*.
- Largest animal phylum: **Arthropoda**. Second largest: **Mollusca**.
- Living fossil: *Limulus*.
- Metagenesis: *Obelia*. High regeneration: *Planaria*.
- Stomochord: Hemichordata.
- Notochord only in larval tail: **Urochordata**; head to tail throughout life: **Cephalochordata**.
- Electric organ: *Torpedo*. Poison sting: *Trygon*.
- Oviparous mammal: *Ornithorhynchus*.

## Practice Questions (NEET pattern)

1. Which one of the following is NOT a defining feature of living organisms?
   - (a) Metabolism
   - (b) Cellular organisation
   - (c) Consciousness
   - (d) Growth
2. The correct sequence of taxonomic categories in ascending order is:
   - (a) Species – Order – Genus – Family
   - (b) Species – Genus – Family – Order
   - (c) Genus – Species – Order – Family
   - (d) Species – Family – Genus – Order
3. Wheat belongs to the order:
   - (a) Sapindales
   - (b) Diptera
   - (c) Polymoniales
   - (d) Poales
4. Which group of archaebacteria is found in the gut of ruminants?
   - (a) Halophiles
   - (b) Thermoacidophiles
   - (c) Methanogens
   - (d) Cyanobacteria
5. The protist with a protein-rich pellicle and two unequal flagella is:
   - (a) *Gonyaulax*
   - (b) *Paramoecium*
   - (c) *Euglena*
   - (d) Diatom
6. In which class of fungi are basidiospores produced exogenously after karyogamy and meiosis?
   - (a) Basidiomycetes
   - (b) Ascomycetes
   - (c) Phycomycetes
   - (d) Deuteromycetes
7. Match the pigment with the algal class: Fucoxanthin – ?
   - (a) Chlorophyceae
   - (b) Phaeophyceae
   - (c) Rhodophyceae
   - (d) Cyanophyceae
8. The precursor to the seed habit is seen in:
   - (a) *Selaginella*
   - (b) *Marchantia*
   - (c) *Funaria*
   - (d) *Dryopteris*
9. Which of the following has an infectious agent made only of abnormally folded protein?
   - (a) Viroid
   - (b) TMV
   - (c) Bacteriophage
   - (d) Prion
10. Flame cells are the excretory structures of:
    - (a) Annelida
    - (b) Platyhelminthes
    - (c) Aschelminthes
    - (d) Arthropoda
11. Which fish lacks an air bladder and has claspers in males?
    - (a) *Catla*
    - (b) *Hippocampus*
    - (c) *Labeo*
    - (d) *Scoliodon*
12. Notochord persists from head to tail throughout life in:
    - (a) *Ascidia*
    - (b) *Branchiostoma*
    - (c) *Petromyzon*
    - (d) *Balanoglossus*

**Answers:** 1–d, 2–b, 3–d, 4–c, 5–c, 6–a, 7–b, 8–a, 9–d, 10–b, 11–d, 12–b
