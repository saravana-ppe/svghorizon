# Cell organelles

## The idea

A cell is not a bag of fluid. It is divided into compartments, each doing one job.
These compartments are the organelles.

## The main organelles

| Organelle | Job |
|---|---|
| Nucleus | Holds DNA, controls the cell |
| Mitochondria | Releases energy as ATP |
| Ribosomes | Builds proteins |
| Endoplasmic reticulum | Transports material inside the cell |
| Golgi apparatus | Packages and sends out material |
| Lysosome | Digests waste and worn out parts |
| Chloroplast | Photosynthesis, in plant cells only |

## Points that are asked often

- Mitochondria and chloroplasts have their own DNA and their own ribosomes
- Lysosomes are called suicide bags because their enzymes can digest the cell itself
- Ribosomes are the only organelle with no membrane around them
