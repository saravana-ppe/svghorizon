**NEET UG 2026 Biology – Notes from NCERT**

**NCERT chapters covered:** Class XI – Chapter 11 (Photosynthesis in Higher Plants), Chapter 12 (Respiration in Plants), Chapter 13 (Plant Growth and Development)

**NEET syllabus topics in this unit:**

1. **Photosynthesis:** as a means of autotrophic nutrition; site of photosynthesis; pigments involved (elementary idea); photochemical and biosynthetic phases; cyclic and non-cyclic photophosphorylation; chemiosmotic hypothesis; photorespiration; C<sub>3</sub> and C<sub>4</sub> pathways; factors affecting photosynthesis
2. **Respiration:** exchange of gases; cellular respiration – glycolysis, fermentation (anaerobic), TCA cycle and electron transport system (aerobic); energy relations – number of ATP molecules generated; amphibolic pathways; respiratory quotient
3. **Plant growth and development:** seed germination; phases of plant growth and plant growth rate; conditions of growth; differentiation, dedifferentiation and redifferentiation; sequence of developmental process in a plant cell; growth regulators – auxin, gibberellin, cytokinin, ethylene, ABA

> **NEET Focus: How this unit is tested**
> - Numerical and sequence questions: ATP/NADPH needed per CO<sub>2</sub> and per glucose in Calvin cycle; ATP from glycolysis and Krebs cycle; RQ values; number of carbons in intermediates.
> - Location questions: where does water splitting happen, where do protons accumulate, which cells have RuBisCO/PEPcase in C<sub>4</sub> plants, where is ETS located.
> - Scientist–experiment matching: Priestley, Ingenhousz, Sachs, Engelmann, van Niel, Calvin, Hatch and Slack, Went, Kurosawa, Skoog and Miller, Cousins.
> - PGR application questions (NCERT Exercise Q8 pattern): which PGR to use for rooting, ripening, delaying senescence, bolting, stomatal closure.

## Quick Revision – High-Yield One-liners

- O<sub>2</sub> in photosynthesis comes from **water** (van Niel; confirmed by radioisotopes).
- First action spectrum – **Engelmann**, *Cladophora*, aerobic bacteria.
- Chlorophyll a – blue green; b – yellow green; xanthophylls – yellow; carotenoids – yellow-orange.
- Accessory pigments protect chlorophyll a from **photo-oxidation**.
- PS I – P700; PS II – P680. Named in order of discovery.
- Water splitting – PS II, inner side of thylakoid; protons and O<sub>2</sub> released into **lumen**.
- Cyclic photophosphorylation – only PS I; only ATP; stroma lamellae; beyond 680 nm light.
- Chemiosmosis in chloroplast – protons accumulate in **lumen**; in mitochondria – in **intermembrane space**.
- CF<sub>0</sub> – channel in membrane; CF<sub>1</sub> – on stroma side, makes ATP.
- First product of C<sub>3</sub> – **PGA**; of C<sub>4</sub> – **OAA**. Acceptor in C<sub>3</sub> – **RuBP (5C)**; in C<sub>4</sub> – **PEP (3C)**.
- Calvin cycle per glucose – **18 ATP, 12 NADPH, 6 turns**.
- Kranz anatomy – bundle sheath; C<sub>4</sub> – maize, sorghum.
- Photorespiration – RuBP + O<sub>2</sub> → phosphoglycerate + **phosphoglycolate (2C)**; no ATP, no sugar.
- Light saturation – **10% of full sunlight**. CO<sub>2</sub> – major limiting factor (0.03–0.04%).
- C<sub>4</sub> saturates at 360 µL L<sup>−1</sup>; C<sub>3</sub> beyond 450 µL L<sup>−1</sup>.
- Glycolysis – EMP; cytoplasm; 10 reactions; net 2 ATP + 2 NADH.
- Invertase – sucrose → glucose + fructose. Hexokinase – glucose → G6P.
- Yeast dies at about 13% alcohol.
- Pyruvate dehydrogenase needs NAD<sup>+</sup>, CoA, Mg<sup>2+</sup>.
- Krebs cycle – starts with citrate synthase; GTP at succinyl-CoA → succinic acid.
- Complex IV – cytochrome c oxidase with Cu centres. Cytochrome c – mobile carrier.
- 1 NADH = 3 ATP; 1 FADH<sub>2</sub> = 2 ATP; 4H<sup>+</sup> per ATP through F<sub>0</sub>.
- Net 38 ATP per glucose (aerobic).
- RQ – carbohydrate 1, fat 0.7, protein 0.9.
- Maize root apical meristem – 17,500 cells per hour; watermelon cells – 3,50,000 times.
- Sigmoid curve – lag, log, stationary.
- Heterophylly – cotton, coriander, larkspur (developmental); buttercup (environmental).
- Auxin – Went, oat coleoptile; GA – Kurosawa, bakanae; kinetin – Skoog and Miller; ethylene – Cousins.

## Practice Questions (NEET pattern)

1. The first action spectrum of photosynthesis was described by:
   - (a) Priestley
   - (b) Engelmann
   - (c) Ingenhousz
   - (d) van Niel
2. Splitting of water is associated with:
   - (a) PS I on the stroma side
   - (b) NADP reductase
   - (c) Cytochrome b<sub>6</sub>f
   - (d) PS II on the inner side of thylakoid membrane
3. Cyclic photophosphorylation results in the formation of:
   - (a) Only ATP
   - (b) Only NADPH
   - (c) ATP and NADPH
   - (d) ATP, NADPH and O<sub>2</sub>
4. How many ATP and NADPH are needed to fix one molecule of CO<sub>2</sub> in the Calvin cycle?
   - (a) 2 ATP, 2 NADPH
   - (b) 18 ATP, 12 NADPH
   - (c) 2 ATP, 3 NADPH
   - (d) 3 ATP, 2 NADPH
5. In C<sub>4</sub> plants, RuBisCO is present in:
   - (a) Mesophyll cells only
   - (b) Bundle sheath cells only
   - (c) Both
   - (d) Guard cells
6. Photorespiration produces:
   - (a) 2 molecules of PGA
   - (b) OAA
   - (c) Phosphoglycerate and phosphoglycolate
   - (d) ATP and NADPH
7. The net gain of ATP in glycolysis (direct) per glucose is:
   - (a) 4
   - (b) 2
   - (c) 8
   - (d) 38
8. Substrate-level phosphorylation in Krebs' cycle occurs during conversion of:
   - (a) Succinyl-CoA to succinic acid
   - (b) Citrate to isocitrate
   - (c) Malate to OAA
   - (d) α-ketoglutarate to succinyl-CoA
9. RQ of tripalmitin is about:
   - (a) 0.7
   - (b) 0.9
   - (c) 1.0
   - (d) 1.33
10. The respiratory pathway is called amphibolic because it:
    - (a) Occurs in both plants and animals
    - (b) Involves both catabolism and anabolism
    - (c) Occurs in both cytoplasm and mitochondria
    - (d) Produces both ATP and NADH
11. Which PGR is used to increase sugarcane yield?
    - (a) Auxin
    - (b) Cytokinin
    - (c) Ethylene
    - (d) Gibberellin
12. Formation of interfascicular cambium from parenchyma cells is an example of:
    - (a) Differentiation
    - (b) Dedifferentiation
    - (c) Redifferentiation
    - (d) Maturation

**Answers:** 1–b, 2–d, 3–a, 4–d, 5–b, 6–c, 7–b, 8–a, 9–a, 10–b, 11–d, 12–b
